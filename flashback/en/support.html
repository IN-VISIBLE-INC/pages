Flashback Support

Flashback creates vertical movie diaries with capture times from the photos and videos you choose. Everything is processed on your device, and no sign-in is required.

FAQ

Are my photos or videos sent anywhere?

No. All loading, editing, and exporting happens on your device. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

What is the time shown in exported videos?

It is the capture date and time recorded in the photo or video. You can also change the time shown in the editor.

I want to remove the watermark

In the free version, a Flashback watermark appears in the lower right of exported videos. Purchase “Flashback Pro” (one-time purchase) from the crown button on the home screen to export without the watermark.

Pro stopped working after I changed devices or reinstalled the app

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase, then tap “Restore Purchases” on the screen that opens from the crown button. You will not be charged again.

I can't save to my camera roll

In iPhone Settings → Apps → Flashback → Photos, check that photo access is not set to “None”.

I want a refund

Payments are managed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple. Please use Apple's Report a Problem page.
