Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

INVISIBLE, Y.K (“we”, “us”) sets out below how we handle user information in the movie diary app “Flashback” (the “App”).

1. Who we are

  • Name: INVISIBLE, Y.K (有限会社インビジブル)
  • Contact: customer_support@in-visible.net
  • Address and name of representative: Provided without delay upon request (please contact us at the address above).

2. Your photos and videos

The App creates vertical movies with capture times from the photos and videos you choose. All loading, editing, and exporting of photos and videos happens on your device. We never send your photos, videos, or their capture dates and times to us or to anyone else.

  • Choosing photos: The App uses the iOS photo picker. The App can read only the photos and videos you choose.
  • Saving to your camera roll: The App asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you save a finished movie or a photo or video taken from a capture reminder.
  • Camera and microphone: The App asks for camera permission, and microphone permission to record audio for videos, only when you take a photo or video from a capture reminder. Photos and videos you take are only saved to your camera roll and are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Capture reminder notifications: When you turn on capture reminders, the App shows notifications prompting you to capture a moment, at the number of times and hours you set. Notifications are scheduled only on your device, and your reminder settings (times per day, quiet hours, and so on) are also stored on your device. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • What is stored on your device: Copies of the photos and videos you chose, thumbnails, capture dates and times, your edits (order, position of the time display, colors, and so on), and your capture reminder settings. Deleting the App deletes all of these.

3. Information we collect

The App has no account registration or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, or location. The App does not use advertising, usage analytics tools, or AI services.

We collect only the following information, which is needed for the in-app purchase (Flashback Pro):

  • An anonymous ID that the App assigns to each device
  • Purchase records (the item purchased, date and time, transaction ID, and so on, received from Apple)
  • Information sent as part of network communication (device model, OS version, app version, IP address, and so on)

Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your credit card or other payment information.

4. How we use information

  • To verify purchases and provide purchased features (exporting without the watermark)
  • To restore purchases with “Restore Purchases” after reinstalling or on another device
  • To respond to your inquiries

5. Sharing with third parties and service providers

We do not provide personal information to third parties without your consent, except as required by law. The App uses the following services and sends them the information described in Section 3.

  • Apple (App Store, StoreKit): Used for in-app purchase payments and purchase verification.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States): Used to verify purchases and manage whether a purchase has been made. We entrust it with storing and processing purchase records. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Provision to third parties in foreign countries

RevenueCat, Inc. is a business in the United States. The United States has no comprehensive federal law equivalent to Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information; personal information is protected by state laws and other regulations. Each country's system can be checked in the Personal Information Protection Commission's survey of personal information protection systems in foreign countries (in Japanese). We limit the information we send to the minimum needed to verify purchases, and we use the service after confirming the protections described in its privacy policy and terms of service.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Copies of photos and videos and your edits stored on your device are deleted when you delete the App.
  • Purchase records are kept to verify and restore purchases. If you would like them deleted, please contact us at the address below.

7. Requests for disclosure, correction, suspension of use, and more

If you request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, or suspension of provision to third parties of personal information we hold about you, we will verify your identity and respond without delay in accordance with applicable law. Please contact us at the address below.

8. Use by minors

If you are a minor, please use the App with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy to reflect changes in law or in the App. If there are important changes, we will let you know in the App.

10. Contact

For questions or requests about how we handle personal information, please contact:

INVISIBLE, Y.K
Email: customer_support@in-visible.net
